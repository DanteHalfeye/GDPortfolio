## Overview

**Trial of the Gods** is a 3D action-platformer developed during **Global Game Jam 2026**.

The game challenges players to navigate an ancient temple built into a canyon, using **divine masks** to unlock new movement abilities. Each mask changes how the player interacts with the environment, turning exploration into a series of movement-based trials.

The project was developed in **2 days**, requiring the team to rapidly prototype, iterate, and polish the core gameplay experience within the constraints of a game jam.

## Core Gameplay

The main gameplay loop revolves around **exploration, ability acquisition, and platforming challenges**.

Players progress through the temple by discovering altars and obtaining divine masks. These masks grant specialized abilities that allow the player to reach previously inaccessible areas and approach obstacles in different ways.

The goal was to make movement itself feel rewarding, with each new ability expanding the player's options for navigating the environment.

### Divine Masks

The mask system acts as the game's main progression mechanic.

#### Wind Mask

The **Wind Mask** focuses on speed and mobility, allowing the player to:

* Sprint at increased speed.
* Perform directional dashes.
* Cross larger gaps.
* Reach higher or otherwise inaccessible platforms.
* Move through the environment more fluidly.

Rather than simply functioning as upgrades, the masks are integrated directly into the level design, making their abilities necessary for overcoming specific environmental challenges.

## Key Features

### Mask-Based Ability System

A dynamic ability system allows players to equip different masks and immediately change their available movement capabilities.

The system connects **UI, player state, and movement parameters**, allowing abilities to be added and modified without rebuilding the entire character controller.

### Movement-Focused Platforming

The platforming was designed around the player's abilities rather than traditional static jumps.

Sprint boosts, dashes, wall movement, and enhanced jumping allow the player to approach obstacles with greater freedom and create a stronger sense of momentum.

### Environmental Altars

Altars act as interaction and progression points throughout the temple.

They introduce new abilities to the player while also providing a visual connection between the game's mechanics and its mythological setting.

## Design Challenges

One of the main challenges was making the additional movement abilities feel **powerful without removing the challenge from the level design**.

For example, giving the player a dash changes the effective distance of every platforming challenge. Instead of treating abilities as simple stat upgrades, we designed the environment around what the player could do with each mask.

This created a progression where obtaining a new mask didn't just make the player stronger — it **changed how the player could interact with the world**.
